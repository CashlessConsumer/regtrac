National Financial Reporting Authority

Companies Act, 2013, section 132 (constituted 2018)

Record

  • Statute: Companies Act, 2013, section 132 (constituted 2018)
  • Established: 2018
  • Under: Ministry of Corporate Affairs
  • Mandate: Audit oversight: auditors and audit firms of listed and large unlisted companies; enforcement of accounting and auditing standards
  • Consumer grievance route: No retail grievance channel — enforcement-led
  • Site: https://nfra.gov.in
