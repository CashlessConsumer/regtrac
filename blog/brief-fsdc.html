The apex body where regulators meet the Finance Minister — with no statute, and largely unpublished minutes.

RegTrac Brief · 2026-09-13 · Non-statutory · context · register as of 12 September 2026

What changed

  • Chairperson: Nirmala Sitharaman (since 2019-05 — Ex officio as Union Finance Minister) (appointment source).
  • Register position as rebuilt on 12 September 2026; every line above carries its own source.

Why it matters

  • FSDC has no statute: it was created by executive resolution in 2010 and is chaired ex officio by the Union Finance Minister.
  • It coordinates financial stability, inter-regulator disputes, financial literacy and inclusion councils, and a cyber security forum — the venue where cross-regulator positions get settled.
  • Because it is non-statutory, RegTrac carries it as flagged context rather than as part of the regulator register.
  • Statute and mandate: No statute — created by executive resolution, 2010 — FSDC official site · RegTrac register entry.

Who lobbies these decisions, and what consultation comments were filed, is the missing third layer — LobbyWatch (planned) — because regulators here do not systematically publish their comment trails; the accountability gaps are catalogued in the RegTrac register.

Who is affected

  • Grievance route: No direct public grievance channel; minutes largely unpublished (FSDC site).
  • The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the RegTrac register entry for FSDC.

What to watch

  • Minutes are largely unpublished, so the coordination that shapes policy is invisible even though the participants are named public bodies.
  • There is no public grievance channel and no statutory route to seek FSDC records.
  • RegTrac's register logs no sourced change at FSDC since it was built.
  • Every watchpoint above is tracked against the RegTrac register entry for FSDC, as rebuilt on 12 September 2026; no line here rests on information the register does not carry.
