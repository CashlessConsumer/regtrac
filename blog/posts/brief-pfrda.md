# Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority — what changed, and what to watch

The youngest of the big-four regulators. PFRDA runs the National Pension System and Atal Pension Yojana — the retirement money of government employees, since 2004, and any citizen who opts in. Its core consumer problem: subscribers take all the market risk, while fund managers take management fees on other people's retirement.

*RegTrac Brief · 2026-09-13 · Statutory regulator · register as of 12 September 2026*

## What changed

- **2025-06-20** — Sivasubramanian Ramann assumes charge as PFRDA Chairperson (20 Jun 2025) ([Leadership source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/leadership-overhaul-in-indian-regulatory-bodies-2025-changes-at-rbi-sebi-irdai-and-pfrda/126188548)).
- **Chairperson:** Sivasubramanian Ramann (since 2025-06-20 — Former Deputy CAG and CTO Office of CAG) ([appointment source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/leadership-overhaul-in-indian-regulatory-bodies-2025-changes-at-rbi-sebi-irdai-and-pfrda/126188548)).
- *Register position as rebuilt on 12 September 2026; every line above carries its own source.*

## Why it matters

- Registration and penalty powers over pension funds, CRAs and PoPs
- Investment-pattern regulations — how much of your money goes to equity, corporate debt, government paper, AIFs
- Subscriber-protection regulations (2022): exit norms, service standards, withdrawal timelines
- Scheme design: the 2025 assured-payout and inflation-indexed NPS products were shaped through expert panels the chairperson convenes
- *Statute and mandate: PFRDA Act, 2013 (statutory w.e.f. 1 Feb 2014; interim body since 2003) — [PFRDA official site](https://www.pfrda.org.in) · [RegTrac register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-pfrda.html).*

Who lobbies these decisions, and what consultation comments were filed, is the missing third layer — LobbyWatch (planned) — because regulators here do not systematically publish their comment trails; the accountability gaps are catalogued in the [RegTrac register](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/regulators.html).

## Who is affected

- **Grievance route:** NPS Ombudsman under PFRDA (Redressal of Subscriber Grievance) Regulations, 2015; CRA helplines ([PFRDA site](https://www.pfrda.org.in)).
- Fund-manager choice matters less than tier and scheme choice: the government default scheme exists for people who do not want to pick.
- At exit, annuity purchase is mandatory for a large share of the corpus — annuity pricing is set by insurers regulated by IRDAI, not PFRDA. Your pension regulator does not control your pension payout.
- *The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the [RegTrac register entry for PFRDA](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-pfrda.html).*

## What to watch

- Pension funds are subsidiaries of the same fund houses and insurers whose products they "compete" on — fee and default-scheme design are the places to watch.
- Product design by expert panel (assured payout, inflation-linked NPS) is good process, but panel composition and submissions are not published.
- Coverage push toward informal workers via UPI-based micro-contributions will make PFRDA a mass-market DPI regulator — consumer protection staffing has not visibly scaled to match.
