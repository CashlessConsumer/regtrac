# National Financial Reporting Authority — what changed, and what to watch

Audit oversight with real enforcement teeth and no door for the public.

*RegTrac Brief · 2026-09-13 · Statutory · adjacent · register as of 12 September 2026*

## What changed

- No sourced change is logged for NFRA since RegTrac's register was rebuilt on 12 September 2026. The brief records the standing position, and the next revision will report the first sourced movement ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-nfra.html)).

## Why it matters

- NFRA was constituted in 2018 under section 132 of the Companies Act, 2013, to oversee auditors and audit firms of listed and large unlisted companies.
- It enforces accounting and auditing standards — its orders land on professionals rather than on consumers directly.
- It is statutory-adjacent in RegTrac's register: a financial-reporting watchdog under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, not the finance ministry's regulatory core.
- *Statute and mandate: Companies Act, 2013, section 132 (constituted 2018) — [NFRA official site](https://nfra.gov.in) · [RegTrac register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-nfra.html).*

Who lobbies these decisions, and what consultation comments were filed, is the missing third layer — LobbyWatch (planned) — because regulators here do not systematically publish their comment trails; the accountability gaps are catalogued in the [RegTrac register](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/regulators.html).

## Who is affected

- **Grievance route:** No retail grievance channel — enforcement-led ([NFRA site](https://nfra.gov.in)).
- *The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the [RegTrac register entry for NFRA](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-nfra.html).*

## What to watch

- NFRA publishes no retail grievance channel; the only path to raise a concern runs through the audit regulator's own process after the fact.
- Enforcement-led oversight means the public learns of audit failure mainly through orders, not through routine disclosure.
- RegTrac's register logs no sourced change at NFRA since it was built.
