# National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development — what changed, and what to watch

Part development bank, part supervisor. NABARD refinances rural credit and runs India's farm-infrastructure funds — and, less visibly, carries statutory supervisory duties over cooperative banks and regional rural banks, the weakest links in India's deposit-safety chain.

*RegTrac Brief · 2026-09-13 · Statutory regulator · register as of 12 September 2026*

## What changed

- **Chairman:** Shaji K V (since 2022-12 — Confirmed in office as of Sep 2026) ([appointment source](https://guidely.in/current-affairs/daily-current-affairs-05-september-2026)).
- *Register position as rebuilt on 12 September 2026; every line above carries its own source.*

## Why it matters

- Refinance volumes as credit steering — NABARD decides which lending models get cheap money
- Supervisory findings that feed RBI action on weak cooperative banks
- Fund administration for most central-sector rural schemes — budget execution power without budget visibility
- *Statute and mandate: NABARD Act, 1981 (established 12 Jul 1982) — [NABARD official site](https://www.nabard.org) · [RegTrac register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-nabard.html).*

Who lobbies these decisions, and what consultation comments were filed, is the missing third layer — LobbyWatch (planned) — because regulators here do not systematically publish their comment trails; the accountability gaps are catalogued in the [RegTrac register](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/regulators.html).

## Who is affected

- **Grievance route:** Customers of supervised institutions use RBI Ombudsman route; NABARD itself handles institutional complaints ([NABARD site](https://www.nabard.org)).
- If you bank with a cooperative bank, your deposit insurance comes from DICGC like anyone else's, but supervision is split between NABARD and RBI — when co-op banks fail, both blame the arrangement.
- RIDF and NABARD-funded infrastructure is public money; project lists are published but outcomes rarely are.
- *The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the [RegTrac register entry for NABARD](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-nabard.html).*

## What to watch

- Cooperative banking supervision is the longest-running accountability muddle in Indian finance — NABARD inspects, RBI licenses, state registrars co-exist; failures (the urban co-op pattern) fall between chairs.
- Appointment and tenure norms for the chairman track the PSU-bank pattern (Centre-decided) rather than an independent process.
