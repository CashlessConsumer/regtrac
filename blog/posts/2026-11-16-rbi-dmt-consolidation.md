For the second Monday running, the week's biggest register move is an RBI consolidation. On 11 November 2026 the Reserve Bank of India issued the Reserve Bank of India (Domestic Money Transfer) Directions, 2026 — circular RBI/DPSS/2026-27/476 — pulling the rules for small-value cash-based remittances into a single text, effective immediately ([RBI notification, 11 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [official PDF](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/476MDDC5A297B05094F8096910FE97E1AD34D.PDF)).

## What changed

The Directions are issued under section 18 read with section 10(2) of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007, and consolidate the extant instructions on Domestic Money Transfer, the framework RBI runs so that people — including migrant workers — who lack proof of identity or address can still move money through formal banking channels. They apply to all scheduled commercial banks — regional rural banks, urban co-operative banks, state co-operative banks and district central co-operative banks included — and to authorised payment system operators and participants; card-to-card transfers are excluded and stay governed by their own instrument rules ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [ETBFSI, 11 Nov 2026](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-consolidates-domestic-money-transfer-rules-specifies-transaction-limits/135738757)). The substance, per the circular text ([TaxGuru republication](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-domestic-money-transfer-directions-2026-set-cash-remittance-limits.html)): the Cash Pay-out Service — bank account to cash delivery for recipients without accounts — is capped at Rs 10,000 per transaction and Rs 25,000 per beneficiary per month, with the remitting bank recording the beneficiary's name and address; a walk-in customer at a branch can remit up to Rs 50,000 to a bank account through NEFT; through business correspondents and ATMs the ceiling is Rs 5,000 per transaction and Rs 25,000 per remitter per month. Remitters must be registered on a verified mobile number and a self-certified officially valid document under the KYC Directions, 2025, every transaction needs an additional factor of authentication, and banks and their BCs must follow the applicable Income-Tax Act, 2025 provisions on cash deposits. Every IMPS or NEFT message must carry the remitter's details and an identifier marking the transfer as a cash-based remittance ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-consolidates-domestic-money-transfer-rules-specifies-transaction-limits/135738757)). Providers must run velocity checks and customer credit alerts, investigate unusual credit spurts immediately and alert authorities about suspicious transactions; transfers are expected to settle in real or near-real time, over RBI-approved payment systems only. Banks may fix reasonable charges, and grievances fall under the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0)). Two earlier circulars — 5 October 2011 on DMT relaxations and 24 July 2024 on the DMT framework review — are subsumed and repealed by name ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-consolidates-domestic-money-transfer-rules-specifies-transaction-limits/135738757)).

## Why it matters

This is the second consolidation circular in a week: [last week's edition](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/blog/2026-11-09-rbi-nonbank-pso-cyber-rulebook.html) covered the non-bank payment operator cyber rulebook (475, 5 November); the domestic money transfer directions are 476, six days later. Business Standard's reading is that the limits are largely carried forward — consolidation, not a reset ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/rbi-consolidates-money-transfer-rules-check-cash-limits-and-kyc-norms-126111300610_1.html)). What is new in force is the traceability layer: remitter identity in the transaction message, a marker separating cash remittances from ordinary transfers, and a monitoring duty that makes unusual credit spurts an investigation trigger ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-consolidates-domestic-money-transfer-rules-specifies-transaction-limits/135738757)). For the customer, one text now states the limits, verification steps and ombudsman route, instead of circulars spread across fifteen years. Elsewhere on the register this week, IRDAI constituted separate life and non-life working groups on 10 November 2026 to converge its risk-based capital framework with Indian accounting standards ([TaxGuru, office order IRDAI/RBC/ORD/MISC/150/11/2026](https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/irdai-constitutes-life-non-life-working-groups-risk-based-capital-ind-convergence.html)).

## Who is affected

The people the framework exists for: migrant workers and others moving money to family without full identity or address documentation, and recipients who collect remittances in cash — capped at Rs 10,000 per transaction and Rs 25,000 a month per beneficiary ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/rbi-consolidates-money-transfer-rules-check-cash-limits-and-kyc-norms-126111300610_1.html)). Walk-in customers sending up to Rs 50,000 by NEFT at a branch have one citable rule ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0)). Compliance lands on scheduled commercial banks, co-operative banks, business correspondents and payment system operators ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-consolidates-domestic-money-transfer-rules-specifies-transaction-limits/135738757)). If a transfer fails or a remittance goes astray: complain to the bank or payment service provider first, then escalate to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026, which the Directions name, through the complaint portal at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in) ([RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0); [register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-rbi.html)). Which industry bodies sought the relaxation windows or charge flexibility in the 2024 review and this consolidation is [LobbyWatch](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in)'s ledger; the self-regulatory layer that borrows these conduct rules back to its members is [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in)'s.

- **IBBI's empty chair, four days out.** Jayanti Prasad holds the Chairperson's post in additional charge until 20 November 2026 or until a successor joins ([Economic Times, Sep 2026](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-board-chairperson/articleshow/134396665.cms)).
- **PSL consolidation comments close 26 November.** RBI's five entity-wise draft directions on priority sector lending — commercial banks, small finance banks, regional rural banks, local area banks, urban co-operative banks — retain the headline targets of 40 per cent, 60 per cent and 75 per cent respectively ([RBI press release](https://m.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925); [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-reviews-priority-sector-lending-framework-retains-broad-targets-in-draft-norms/135578794)).
