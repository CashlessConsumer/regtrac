# Securities and Exchange Board of India — what changed, and what to watch

The markets regulator. Everything listed, everything pooled, everything sold as an investment: brokers, mutual funds, AIFs, IPOs, takeover codes, insider trading. SEBI writes the rules, licenses the players, investigates, and adjudicates — all three powers in one body, which is both its strength and its standing conflict-of-interest.

*RegTrac Brief · 2026-09-13 · Statutory regulator · register as of 12 September 2026*

- **2025-12-01** — Sandip Pradhan inducted as SEBI Whole-Time Member (Dec 2025) ([Leadership source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/leadership-overhaul-in-indian-regulatory-bodies-2025-changes-at-rbi-sebi-irdai-and-pfrda/126188548)).
- **2025-02-06** — Tuhin Kanta Pandey appointed SEBI Chairperson (Feb 2025), succeeding Madhabi Puri Buch ([Leadership source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/leadership-overhaul-in-indian-regulatory-bodies-2025-changes-at-rbi-sebi-irdai-and-pfrda/126188548)).
- **Chairperson:** Tuhin Kanta Pandey (since 2025-02, succeeding Madhabi Puri Buch) ([appointment source](https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/boards-policy-regulation/india-watchdog-presses-banks-other-regulators-stricter-insider-trading-2026-03-02)).
- **Whole-Time Member:** Sandip Pradhan (since 2025-12) ([appointment source](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/leadership-overhaul-in-indian-regulatory-bodies-2025-changes-at-rbi-sebi-irdai-and-pfrda/126188548)).

## Why it matters

- Search, seizure, summons, disgorgement, settlement without admission; market-wide bans on bad actors
- T+1 settlement made India the first large market to run it (Jan 2023); optional T+0 beta since 2024
- Disclosure regimes it keeps re-tightening after every episode of promoter abuse
- *Statute and mandate: SEBI Act, 1992 (statutory; existed as non-statutory body since 1988) — [SEBI official site](https://www.sebi.gov.in) · [RegTrac register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-sebi.html).*

SEBI-recognised SROs (AMFI, ANMI, BASL) sit in SROTrac's future scope: [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in).

## Who is affected

- **Grievance route:** SCORES 2.0 — scores.gov.in; SMART ODR for online dispute resolution ([SEBI site](https://www.sebi.gov.in)).
- SEBI's own studies have repeatedly found most retail F&O traders lose money — Reuters reported the pattern again in Mar 2026. The regulator knows the house odds and says so; the products are still on sale.
- Check adviser/broker registration on SEBI's site before paying anyone for "stock tips".
- *The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the [RegTrac register entry for SEBI](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-sebi.html).*

## What to watch

- The previous chair's 2024-25 term ended amid unprecedented public scrutiny of the chair's personal disclosures — the appointment process itself (ACC, no public shortlist) was the structural weakness everyone pointed at.
- Consultation papers are public; who responds, and which responses get accepted, is not systematically published — the LobbyWatch gap.
- Industry-standard setters (AMFI, BASL, ANMI) are SEBI-recognised SROs — industry writing its own code again; their rosters are a future SROTrac extension.
