# Reserve Bank of India — what changed, and what to watch

The oldest and most powerful financial regulator in India — and the one that touches the most people. The RBI licenses and supervises banks, NBFCs and payment systems, sets interest-rate policy through the Monetary Policy Committee, and runs the ombudsman scheme that is most Indians' only practical route to compensation when a bank or wallet loses their money.

## Why it matters

- Issue, suspend and cancel licences; impose moratoria (the Yes Bank moratorium, 2020, is the template)
- Monetary policy: statutory inflation target of 4% (+/- 2%) under the 2016 amendments; MPC rate decisions
- Directions and master directions — the real rulebook; everything from UPI transaction limits to NBFC conduct
- Recognition of self-regulatory organisations: the SRO-FT framework (draft Jan 2024) produced FACE (Aug 2024) and UFF (Sep 2026) — tracked in [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in)
- Depositor protection lever: ₹5 lakh deposit insurance via DICGC (a wholly owned subsidiary)
- *Statute and mandate: Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934 (operational 1 Apr 1935) — [RBI official site](https://www.rbi.org.in) · [RegTrac register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-rbi.html).*

The RBI *recognises* India's fintech SROs — FACE and UFF — so who sits inside them decides how industry consensus forms: [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in) tracks those rosters.

## Who is affected

- **Grievance route:** RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme — cms.rbi.org.in (free; escalate after 30 days of no reply from the regulated entity) ([RBI site](https://www.rbi.org.in)).
- Zero-MDR on UPI and debit cards is a statutory mandate (from Jan 2020) — if a merchant refuses UPI or adds a "UPI charge", that is a violation, not a policy.
- Unclaimed deposits sit in RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness fund — the RBI runs drives to return them; check your family's dormant accounts.
- *The full route, with the register's own notes on escalation, is in the [RegTrac register entry for RBI](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-rbi.html).*

## What to watch

- The top jobs are central-government appointments; the Board mix is government-nominated. Leadership churn (2024-25) happened entirely through ACC decisions.
- The SRO framework formally hands industry consensus formation (fees, codes, grievance standards) to industry bodies — [SROTrac tracks who those members are](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in).
- Consultation practice: draft master directions invite comments, but the RBI does not systematically publish who commented or what changed — the LobbyWatch gap.
