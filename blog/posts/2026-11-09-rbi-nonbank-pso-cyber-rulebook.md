The week's biggest move came from consolidation. On 5 November 2026 the Reserve Bank of India issued a single rulebook for the cybersecurity of the companies that move the country's non-bank money ([RBI notifications page, 5 Nov 2026 entry](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx)).

## What changed

RBI issued the Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank Payment System Operators, circular RBI/DPSS/2026-27/475, dated 5 November 2026, under section 10(2) read with section 18 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 ([notification PDF](https://rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/475MDA65A2A40240B4510BB6301A33C9ECBFE.PDF); [full text via TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). It consolidates the Master Directions of 30 July 2024 with the cyber-security controls for third-party ATM switch application service providers (2019) and the system-audit directions for payment systems (2020), naming the circulars it repeals ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). The directions apply to every authorised non-bank PSO — prepaid payment instrument issuers, payment aggregators, card networks, white-label ATM operators — and extend to unregulated ecosystem entities, such as payment gateways and vendors, through contractual adherence under a Board-approved policy ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). Large and medium PSOs must comply from the date of placement on the RBI website; small PSOs from 1 April 2028 ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html); [Informist, 5 Nov 2026](https://www.informistmedia.com/EquityWire/64254/Cyber-Resilience-RBI-tightens-cybersecurity-norms-for-non-bank-payment-operators)). The substance spans governance (Board oversight, annually reviewed information-security policy, cyber crisis management plan, a Chief Information Security Officer), baseline technical controls, and an annual System Audit Report due within two months of the financial-year close, with unresolved observations ordinarily corrected within three months ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). Incident reporting is clocked: cyberattacks, critical outages, internal fraud and settlement delays must reach RBI within six hours of detection (cyber incidents also to CERT-In), and audit logs are preserved for at least five years ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html); [PSU Watch, 6 Nov 2026](https://www.psuindia.com/breaking-news/rbi-tightens-cybersecurity-regulations-for-non-bank-payment-operators-mandates-six-hour-incident-reporting-561955)). The same text carries consumer-facing controls: a minimum 12-hour cooling period before online payments are allowed after a change to the registered mobile number or email linked to a payment instrument, and transaction display rules — merchant name and amount, or beneficiary name and debit amount for transfers, taken from the entity maintaining the beneficiary account ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html); [Informist](https://www.informistmedia.com/EquityWire/64254/Cyber-Resilience-RBI-tightens-cybersecurity-norms-for-non-bank-payment-operators)).

## Why it matters

Consolidation is not housekeeping; it relocates accountability. Obligations spread across three instruments issued between 2019 and 2024 now live in one checkable text with the repeals named, so a customer, auditor or journalist can check what an operator owes in one place ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). RBI issued a parallel technology-rulebook consolidation for banks and regulated entities on 31 July 2026 ([KPMG, Sep 2026](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmgsites/in/pdf/2026/09/rbis-technology-focused-master-directions-issued-on-31-july-2026.pdf)); non-bank payment operators now have theirs. It lands three weeks before the deadline in [last week's edition](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/blog/2026-11-02-sc-supervises-rbi-kyc.html): the Supreme Court is supervising RBI's KYC Amendment Directions, due to be notified by 30 November 2026. Payment rule-writing is moving on two fronts: conduct rules under court supervision, a security rulebook by consolidation. NPCI, tracked in this register as non-statutory context, is an RBI-authorised non-bank payment system operator ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-npci.html)), so the consolidated directions reach it too ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)).

## Who is affected

Everyone who pays through a non-bank operator — wallet and PPI users, merchants on payment aggregators, card users, white-label ATM customers. The 12-hour cooling period after a registered mobile or email change closes a takeover path: someone who seizes a victim's number cannot immediately transact online on the linked instrument ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). The display rules make payment notifications carry the merchant or beneficiary name, so a customer can see who actually received the money ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). Customers of small PSOs wait longer for the full protection — they comply from 1 April 2028 ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)). If a payment fails or fraud is suspected: complain to the bank or payment service provider first, escalate through NPCI's dispute channels, then take unresolved complaints to the RBI Integrated Ombudsman at [cms.rbi.org.in](https://cms.rbi.org.in) ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-npci.html)). Which industry bodies pressed for speed or carve-outs during the consolidation is [LobbyWatch](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in)'s ledger; the industry and self-regulatory layer that borrows these conduct rules back is [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in)'s.

## What to watch

- **The small-operator runway.** Whether small non-bank PSOs reach compliance by 1 April 2028 or the deadline migrates again — the 2024 framework already set the same date ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)).
- **The first audit cycle.** System Audit Reports for the current financial year are due within two months of its close; auditor classes and corrective-action timelines are specified in the text ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-2026-cyber-resilience-digital-payment-security-directions-non-bank-psos.html)).
- **IBBI's empty chair.** Jayanti Prasad holds the Chairperson's post in additional charge until 20 November 2026 or until a successor joins ([Economic Times, Sep 2026](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-board-chairperson/articleshow/134396665.cms)).
- **SEBI's settlement reset.** The SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026 apply from 7 November 2026, replacing the 2018 Regulations; the first settlements under the new framework will show how its timelines work in practice ([SEBI FAQ, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/faqfiles/nov-2026/1793959419974.pdf)).
