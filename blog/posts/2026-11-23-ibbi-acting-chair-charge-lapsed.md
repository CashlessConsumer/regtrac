Last week this desk put a dated fuse on the register's longest-running open question: Jayanti Prasad's additional charge as Chairperson of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India was set to run "until 20 November 2026 or until a successor is appointed and joins" ([last week's edition](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/blog/2026-11-16-rbi-dmt-consolidation.html); [register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)). The fuse has burned to its end, and nothing public has replaced it.

## What changed

Ravi Mital's tenure ended in mid-September 2026, and Jayanti Prasad — a Whole-Time Member — took additional charge as Chairperson from 13 September 2026, time-bound to run until 20 November 2026 or until a successor joins ([The Economic Times, 22 Sep 2026](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-board-chairperson/articleshow/134396665.cms); [GKToday, 22 Sep 2026](https://www.gktoday.in/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-ibbi-chairperson)). The government opened the search for a full-time Chairperson in June 2026, while Mital was still in office ([Business Today, 8 Jun 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/ibbi-initiates-search-for-next-chairperson-applications-open-as-succession-process-starts-early-535500-2026-06-08)). The only council-level, on-record confirmation of the acting arrangement came at the 30th FSDC meeting on 23 October 2026, whose attendance list names "Shri Jayanti Prasad, Acting Chairperson" ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3); [this desk's edition of 26 Oct](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/blog/2026-10-26-fsdc-30th-meeting-ibbi-acting-chair.html)). The stated end date arrived on Friday. As of 22 November 2026, the Board's whats-new page — last updated 16 November 2026 — lists nothing after that date but resolution-plan approvals, and its homepage still carries only the September additional-charge notice; no successor and no extension had been announced there, or in the press checked for this edition ([IBBI whats-new](https://ibbi.gov.in/en/whats-new); [IBBI homepage](https://ibbi.gov.in/)).

## Why it matters

IBBI referees every corporate insolvency under the Code — the process through which a failed company's homebuyers, workers and suppliers are paid or are not ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)). In July 2026 the Calcutta High Court held that the Chairperson cannot act as the Board's Disciplinary Committee under the Code ([LiveLaw, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.livelawbiz.com/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-ibbi-chairperson-cannot-act-as-disciplinary-committee-under-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-code-sashi-agarwal-541570)) — a live structural question that will land on whoever takes the chair substantively. Between June and November the succession went from open search to dated acting arrangement to no announced next step; accountability for the referee attaches at the appointment — who is selected, on what published criteria ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)). The timing sharpens the point: on 16 November 2026 SEBI issued informal guidance that a merchant banker doing valuation work must register as a valuer with IBBI under Section 247 of the Companies Act, 2013 ([CorpLaw Updates](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/sebi-informal-guidance-merchant-bankers-ibbi-valuer-registration)) — the insolvency regulator's valuer register grows more load-bearing even as its own leadership lapses.

## Who is affected

Homebuyers, workers, small suppliers waiting on resolutions under the Code, and creditors whose recoveries depend on a functioning referee are the first-order stakeholders ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)). Insolvency professionals and their agencies face the Board's disciplinary oversight; the agencies that borrow conduct rules back to their members are tracked one layer over at [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in). Anyone with a grievance against the Board's regulated ecosystem has a documented channel: the Grievance and Complaint Handling Regulations, amended in February 2026, govern complaints against insolvency professionals, agencies and information utilities ([IBBI Grievance and Complaint Handling (Amendment) Regulations, 2026](https://ibbi.gov.in/uploads/whatsnew/2b9fe26449e9960a2dadf081cb3d2dd3.pdf)); unresolved matters can be escalated to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)).

## What to watch

- **A successor, or an extension.** Any appointment order or charge arrangement will surface on [IBBI's whats-new page](https://ibbi.gov.in/en/whats-new) or via PIB; the register entry updates the day it appears ([register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)).
- **The court-supervised KYC deadline.** RBI's KYC Amendment Directions, 2026 are due to be notified by 30 November 2026 and operationalised by 31 December 2026, with compensation where an institution's due diligence failed ([Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/supreme-court/rbi-rules-must-banks-compensate-victims-not-less-amount-lost-due-diligence-fails-1624758); [this desk's edition of 2 Nov](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/blog/2026-11-02-sc-supervises-rbi-kyc.html)).
- **PSL draft comments close 26 November.** RBI's five entity-wise draft directions on priority sector lending remain open for comment until then ([RBI press release](https://m.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925)).
