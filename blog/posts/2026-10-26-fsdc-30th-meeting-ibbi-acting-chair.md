The register's coordination layer met this week. On 23 October 2026 the Financial Stability and Development Council — FSDC, the Finance-Minister-chaired body that sits above every entity on this register — held its 30th meeting in New Delhi. Two things make it the week's consequential development: the agenda reads like a preview of the next year of rule-writing across the register, and the government's own attendance list closed the register's longest-running open question — who runs the insolvency regulator.

## What changed

The Council reviewed macro-financial stability amid rising geopolitical uncertainties, an AI boom and a sharp rise in global bond yields, and set a wide workplan: promoting fintech innovation and exploring the tokenisation of financial and real-world assets; taking note of a Financial Sector Cyber Security Strategy formulated by an inter-ministerial group of regulators and cybersecurity agencies with the finance ministries and MeitY; protecting critical information infrastructure and improving regional rural banks' cybersecurity preparedness; simplifying and digitalising KYC for NRIs and FPIs; scaling the Account Aggregator and Unified Lending Interface frameworks with an MSME focus; cutting intermediation costs; and rationalising credit-guarantee schemes ([PIB, 23 Oct 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)). The Finance Minister also asked regulators to expedite refunds of unclaimed assets, and urged more frequent meetings on a predefined schedule ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)).

Then the seating. The official attendee list records "Shri Jayanti Prasad, Acting Chairperson, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India" ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)) — the first council-level confirmation of an arrangement this register has tracked since June. Ravi Mital's tenure ended in mid-September when he turned 65, his appointment having run five years or until age 65, whichever came earlier; Prasad, a Whole-Time Member of the board, took additional charge from 13 September 2026 and holds it until his own board tenure completes on 20 November 2026 or a successor is appointed and joins ([The Economic Times, 22 Sep 2026](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-board-chairperson/articleshow/134396665.cms)). The government opened the search for a full-time chairperson back in June 2026; no substantive appointment has been announced ([Business Today, 8 Jun 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/ibbi-initiates-search-for-next-chairperson-applications-open-as-succession-process-starts-early-535500-2026-06-08)). The [IBBI register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html) now records the acting arrangement.

## Why it matters

FSDC has no statute and issues no rules — the register flags it as context, not a rule-writer. But its agenda is where the rule-writers' next files get sketched: tokenisation, a financial-sector cyber strategy and KYC digitalisation each tend to return later as consultation papers, circulars and regulations at SEBI, RBI and IRDAI. Reading the council's list is the cheapest way to know what this register will be tracking next quarter ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)).

The seating matters on its own. IBBI referees every corporate resolution under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code — the process through which homebuyers, employees and small suppliers of a failed company are paid or are not. In July 2026 the Calcutta High Court held that the chairperson cannot act as the board's Disciplinary Committee under the Code ([LiveLaw, 16 Jul 2026](https://www.livelawbiz.com/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-ibbi-chairperson-cannot-act-as-disciplinary-committee-under-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-code-sashi-agarwal-541570)); that structural question now waits on a legislature answer and will land on whoever takes the chair substantively. In the meantime the chair's own position is temporary, with a charge that lapses on a stated date — an accountability gap with a deadline ([register](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)).

## Who is affected

Homebuyers with flats in insolvency and workers with unpaid dues — the classes the Code's repayment waterfall exists to protect first — deal with a board in temporary leadership. Their routes are unchanged: complaints against insolvency professionals and agencies go through IBBI's grievance and complaint mechanism, and claims inside a resolution run through the resolution professional and the NCLT ([IBBI register entry](https://regtrac.cashlessconsumer.in/reg-ibbi.html)). Holders of unclaimed deposits, dividends, shares and insurance proceeds got a direct nod this week — the Finance Minister asked for faster refunds, building on the "Your Money, Your Right" district campaign; the practical route runs through your bank, insurer or pension fund ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)). NRIs and FPIs facing onboarding friction are the named beneficiaries of the KYC simplification push ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)).

## What to watch

- **The substantive appointment, weeks away at most.** The acting charge ends on 20 November 2026 or when a successor joins ([The Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/india/jayanti-prasad-gets-additional-charge-as-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-board-chairperson/articleshow/134396665.cms)). RegTrac will log the appointment in the leadership table on gazette confirmation; what deserves equal scrutiny is whether the criteria and shortlist behind the search opened in June are ever published ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/ibbi-initiates-search-for-next-chairperson-applications-open-as-succession-process-starts-early-535500-2026-06-08)).
- **The Disciplinary Committee answer.** The Calcutta High Court's July holding needs a legislative or regulatory response; watch whether it arrives before or after a new chairperson takes office ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelawbiz.com/high-court/calcutta-high-court/calcutta-high-court-ibbi-chairperson-cannot-act-as-disciplinary-committee-under-insolvency-and-bankruptcy-code-sashi-agarwal-541570)).
- **FSDC's own cadence.** More frequent meetings on a predefined schedule is now the stated position — the date of the next sitting is the test ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)).
- **The council's list becoming the regulators' list.** Tokenisation, the cyber strategy and credit-guarantee rationalisation should surface as consultations; who comments on them is [LobbyWatch](https://lobbywatch.cashlessconsumer.in)'s beat, and the industry bodies that borrow the resulting conduct rules are [SROTrac](https://srotrac.cashlessconsumer.in)'s ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2327596&lang=1&reg=3)).
