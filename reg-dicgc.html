Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation

DICGC Act, 1961 (corporation constituted 1978)

Record

  • Statute: DICGC Act, 1961 (corporation constituted 1978)
  • Established: 1978
  • Under: Reserve Bank of India (wholly owned)
  • Mandate: Deposit insurance: Rs 5 lakh per depositor per bank per right (since 4 Feb 2020); insurer of last resort on bank failure
  • Consumer grievance route: Claims routed via failed banks; RBI Ombudsman for related disputes
  • Site: https://dicgc.org.in
