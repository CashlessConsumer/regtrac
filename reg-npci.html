National Payments Corporation of India

Section 8 not-for-profit company (2008), founded by RBI and IBA — not statutory

Record

  • Statute: Section 8 not-for-profit company (2008), founded by RBI and IBA — not statutory
  • Established: 2008
  • Under: Not a government body
  • Mandate: Runs retail payment rails: UPI, IMPS, NACH, RuPay, AePS, NETC — as an RBI-authorised payment system operator under PSS Act 2007
  • Consumer grievance route: Escalate: PSP/bank first, then NPCI, then RBI Ombudsman (covers UPI failures)
  • Site: https://www.npci.org.in
