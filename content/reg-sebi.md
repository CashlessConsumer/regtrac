# Securities and Exchange Board of India

The markets regulator. Everything listed, everything pooled, everything sold as an investment: brokers, mutual funds, AIFs, IPOs, takeover codes, insider trading. SEBI writes the rules, licenses the players, investigates, and adjudicates — all three powers in one body, which is both its strength and its standing conflict-of-interest.

## Statute and setup

- Set up in 1988 as a non-statutory body; given teeth by the **SEBI Act, 1992** after the Harshad Mehta securities scam.
- Quasi-legislative (regulations), quasi-executive (investigation) and quasi-judicial (orders) powers in one institution.
- Board: Chairperson + Whole-Time Members + RBI Deputy Governor + ministry joint secretary + part-time members. Chair and WTMs are central-government appointments.

## What it regulates

- Stock brokers, depositories, exchanges and clearing corporations
- Mutual funds, AIFs, PMS, portfolio managers, investment advisers and research analysts
- Listed companies (LODR), takeovers, insider trading, buybacks and delisting
- Foreign portfolio investors; credit rating agencies; debenture trustees; online bond platforms

## Powers that matter

- Search, seizure, summons, disgorgement, settlement without admission; market-wide bans on bad actors
- T+1 settlement made India the first large market to run it (Jan 2023); optional T+0 beta since 2024
- Disclosure regimes it keeps re-tightening after every episode of promoter abuse

## Leadership

*(as of 12 Sep 2026 — Chairperson: Tuhin Kanta Pandey since Feb 2025, succeeding Madhabi Puri Buch; Whole-Time Member Sandip Pradhan inducted Dec 2025. Pandey told Reuters in Mar 2026 he had spent his first year in 77 meetings with foreign investors, easing entry rules.)*

## For the person paying

- **Grievance route:** SCORES 2.0 — scores.gov.in — mandatory online escalation for everything from broker payout delays to mutual fund NAV disputes; SMART ODR offers online conciliation and arbitration.
- SEBI's own studies have repeatedly found most retail F&O traders lose money — Reuters reported the pattern again in Mar 2026. The regulator knows the house odds and says so; the products are still on sale.
- Check adviser/broker registration on SEBI's site before paying anyone for "stock tips".

## Watchpoints (capture and accountability)

- The previous chair's 2024-25 term ended amid unprecedented public scrutiny of the chair's personal disclosures — the appointment process itself (ACC, no public shortlist) was the structural weakness everyone pointed at.
- Consultation papers are public; who responds, and which responses get accepted, is not systematically published — the LobbyWatch gap.
- Industry-standard setters (AMFI, BASL, ANMI) are SEBI-recognised SROs — industry writing its own code again; their rosters are a future SROTrac extension.
